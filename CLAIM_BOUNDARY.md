# Claim Boundary

**Supported claim:** On this frozen 80/20 split, the linear and Ridge models predict the diabetes progression target substantially better than the training-mean baseline, with Ridge providing only a very small improvement over ordinary linear regression.

**Not supported:** causal claims, clinical recommendations, or claims of generalization to new hospitals/populations without external validation.

**Diagnostic caution:** the Breusch–Pagan result suggests non-constant residual variance, and BMI slices show unequal error levels. These findings limit confidence in a simple constant-variance regression interpretation.
